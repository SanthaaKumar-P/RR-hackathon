SUPERR SELECTOR - PRESSURE PERFORMANCE RANKING METHODOLOGY
===========================================================================

1. COMPETITION SEGMENTATION
T20 and T20I are ranked independently.

2. PRESSURE DEFINITION
Only Moderate, High and Extreme pressure deliveries are included. First innings uses first_innings_pressure; second innings uses chase_pressure.

3. SAMPLE ELIGIBILITY
Batters: minimum 100 pressure balls and 10 pressure matches.
Bowlers: minimum 60 pressure legal balls and 10 pressure matches.

4. BATTER BASELINE WEIGHTS
strike_rate: 25%
runs_per_match: 20%
runs_per_dismissal: 15%
dot_percentage: 15%
consistency: 25%

5. BOWLER BASELINE WEIGHTS
wickets_per_100: 25%
economy: 20%
dot_percentage: 15%
consistency: 20%
wickets_per_match: 20%

6. NORMALIZATION
Metrics are converted to percentile scores within eligible players of the same format. Lower-is-better metrics are reverse-ranked. Missing components are excluded and remaining weights are renormalized.

7. BATTER CONSISTENCY
60% of the percentage of pressure matches with 10+ runs and 40% of the percentage with 20+ runs.

8. BOWLER CONSISTENCY
Only matches containing at least one valid pressure legal delivery are included. Consistency is calculated as 70% of the percentage of these matches with 1+ credited wicket and 30% of the percentage with 2+ credited wickets. The same corrected match count is used for bowler match eligibility and wickets per match.

9. BOWLER WICKET CREDIT
Bowled, Caught, LBW, Stump and Hit Wicket are credited to bowlers. Run-outs and other non-bowler dismissals are excluded.

10. BOWLER EXTRAS ACCOUNTING
Scenario A: Batter runs plus extras1 for wides and oversteps. Unverified extras_2_runs and other extras are not charged.
Scenario B: Scenario A plus extras_2_runs on oversteps. This is a sensitivity test, not a verified official scoring interpretation.

11. WEIGHT SENSITIVITY
Baseline rankings are compared against volume-focused, efficiency-focused, wicket-impact and control-focused alternative weighting scenarios.

12. INTERPRETATION LIMITATIONS
This is a project-defined analytical model, not an official Rajasthan Royals selection formula. Pressure definitions, delivery classification, sample thresholds and weights are project assumptions. The source does not define extras_2_runs sufficiently to establish its bowler-accounting treatment.
